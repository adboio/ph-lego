MAX THE POSTHOG HEDGEHOG - REVISED FACE

3D building guide: max/instructions/index.html
Printable instructions: max/instructions.pdf
Editable model: max/model.json and max/model.ldr
Four views: max/renders/
Inventory: max/parts.csv

873 pieces, 44 part/color combinations, 282 steps, five colors.
Approximately 18.9 cm wide x 11.8 cm deep x 18.9 cm tall.
Skin remains Tan. The body, coat, arms and feet are unchanged.
The face has centered thin ears, a compact curved muzzle, a brown nose,
and black round eyes with white quarter-tile highlights. No stickers required.

Open the HTML guide in a browser. Its 3D viewer and navigation work offline
when the surrounding folders stay together. Drag to rotate, scroll/pinch to
zoom, and right-drag to pan. Use Show to switch between the complete model
and current step; Reset view fits the model. Step illustration shows the render.

ORDERING
For genuine LEGO, open lego-order/index.html. Its catalog-audited lists contain
976 pieces / 39 combinations. Five Dark Brown part types are replaced with
smaller LEGO parts occupying the same spaces; follow the substitution sheet.
All revised face parts are included. Import only one complete wanted list.

For BrickWith/GoBricks, use brickwith-upload/max-brickwith.ldr or its XML.
Live LDraw import on October 2, 2026: 43 combinations / 871 pieces available,
with an available-parts subtotal of USD 72.06 before shipping/tax.
Two Tan 60474 ear backing plates are unavailable there. For a mixed build,
source these through brickwith-upload/missing-ear-plates-bricklink.xml.
The complete genuine LEGO lists already include these plates.
See brickwith-upload/README.txt and import-confirmed.png for details.
No cart or order was created. Prices and availability can change.

The display model.ldr uses custom colors; the supplier file uses standard
LDraw colors. brickwith-parts.csv is a readable reference, not an upload file.
GoBricks color IDs: 031 Tan, 081 Reddish Brown, 082 Dark Brown, 080 Black,
090 White. Preserve these IDs as text.

DESIGN AND CHECKS
The seated pose follows the supplied plush photographs. This is a stylized
brick interpretation. The extended toolchain checks conservative part envelopes,
connection alignment, assembly order, insertion envelopes and final connectivity.
Seventeen engine tests pass. Four renders and representative early, middle,
facial, late and inventory pages were reviewed.
This is a digital prototype, not a physically tested build. Support/foundation
warnings remain. Clutch strength, tipping and load capacity are not verified.
Foundation plates stay loose until the crossing layers join them.

EDITING
The source uses the local max-1.0 extension implemented in working/engine.
It supports the slopes, curves, round parts and sideways connections used here.
Renders, LDraw, the viewer, inventory and instructions share the same assembly.
Generate a revision in a new output directory and verify it before publication.
The installed brick-models skill and separate lego project were not changed.
